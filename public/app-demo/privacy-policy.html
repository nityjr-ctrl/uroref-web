UroRef — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

UroRef is a free educational reference application designed for urology trainees. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data UroRef collects (very little) and how it is handled.

Data Collection

UroRef does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

• We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
• We do not collect location data.
• We do not use third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising SDKs. Optional anonymous usage counts go only to UroRef's own server (see below).
• We do not require account creation or login.
• We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or any other device features.

Anonymous Usage Counts

To understand which parts of UroRef are useful and what is missing, the app sends small batches of anonymous events to UroRef's own server (a Cloudflare Worker run by the developer): which screen or topic was opened, which calculator or operative guide page was used, whether Ask Ariadne was used and whether it failed, and a search term that returned no results (only when it consists of plain words). These events carry the app version, platform and hour of day. They carry no device identifier, account, cookie, IP address, Ask Ariadne question, SBAR text, stent log or anything about a patient, and cannot be linked to you. Counts are kept for 120 days and used only to improve the app. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Anonymous usage counts; "Clear on-device data" also discards anything not yet sent.

Stent Tracker Feature

The stent tracker feature allows you to log patient stent information locally on your device. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to any server. If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted. It is your responsibility to ensure patient data is handled in accordance with your local trust's information governance policies and the Data Protection Act 2018 / UK GDPR. We recommend using hospital identifiers rather than patient names.

Third-Party Links

UroRef contains links to external websites including BAUS (British Association of Urological Surgeons), MDCalc, and the BURST Urology MIMIC Calculator. These websites have their own privacy policies which we do not control.

Medical Disclaimer

UroRef is intended for educational quick reference only and does not constitute formal clinical guidance. Clinical decisions should not be based solely on information within this app. Always follow your local trust protocols and discuss management with senior colleagues. The developer accepts no liability for clinical decisions made using this application.

Children's Privacy

UroRef is designed for medical professionals and trainees. It is not intended for use by children under 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
