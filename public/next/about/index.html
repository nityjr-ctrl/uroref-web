About

It started as a personal tool.

Built on a urology on-call, by someone who kept running out of time to look things up.

01The story

The story

I'm Nity, a urology registrar in the North West of England.I'm Nity, a urology registrar in the North West of England. UroRef began as a set of notes for myself: the doses I kept re-checking, the pathways I kept half-remembering, the numbers I never had to hand when the phone went at 2am.

The notes became an app because a phone is what you have on nights. Then colleagues asked for it, so it had to be tidier, and referenced, and honest about what it does not know.

Everything since has followed the same shape. One clinical problem, one focused tool, built in the gaps between clinics and lists.

The rule

If it does not save a trainee time on a real shift, it does not go in.

03How it is built

Written by one clinician, and it says so.

Sources are named. Limits are stated. Nothing here is a substitute for your local guideline or your consultant.

Sources

Referenced

Content is drawn from BAUS, EAU and NICE guidance, with the source named on the page it sits on.

Review

Kept current

Reviewed as guidance changes. Where something is contested, the app says that rather than picking a side.

Data

Nothing leaves

The core needs no account and stores no patient data. Ariadne is optional, online, and off unless you turn it on.

UroRef is a reference aid for clinicians in training. It does not replace local guidelines, senior review or clinical judgement. Do not enter patient-identifiable information into Ariadne.
