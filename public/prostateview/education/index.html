How the module works

One teaching loop from walkthrough to recall.

  1. Use the guided walkthroughIntroduce base, mid-gland, apex and zones before quiz mode.
  2. Explore the linked 3D viewerMove between the synthetic 3D gland, axial slice and sagittal reference.
  3. Answer case-based questionsSelect an answer and let the viewer jump to the linked teaching state.
  4. Review mistakes in contextRead the explanation while the relevant overlay, slice or side labels are visible.

Safety by design

Educational orientation only.

No patient-identifiable material is shown in this module. The case is a synthetic teaching case and the target is not a real lesion.

The module does not assign PI-RADS, stage disease, guide biopsy, recommend treatment or support triage.

Side labels and convention warnings stay visible for side-specific teaching so learners do not rely on screen left/right alone.
