# Template Gland Attribution

The ProstateView template gland in this folder is derived from one de-identified ProstateZones segmentation selected as a representative teaching exemplar.

Source datasets:

- ProstateZones: Holmlund W, et al. Scientific Data 11, 1097 (2024). DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10718469.
- PROSTATEx (TCIA): Litjens et al.

The derived template assets are for teaching and spatial orientation only. They are not for diagnosis, navigation, treatment planning, or patient-specific interpretation.

Raw research data and NIfTI inputs are intentionally excluded from git.
