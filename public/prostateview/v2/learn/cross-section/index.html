Prostate Imaging Spatial Drill

Cross-Section Trainer

A cutting plane transects the solid below. Select the two-dimensional section it produces. Inferring the section a plane creates is the core skill of multiparametric MRI: every axial, sagittal and coronal image is a cross-section the reader must place back into the gland.

Which section does this plane produce?

 

Evidence base. Task paradigm after the Santa Barbara Solids Test (Cohen & Hegarty, 2012, Learning and Individual Differences). Cross-sectioning ability, sometimes termed penetrative thinking, predicts performance in anatomy and imaging-based disciplines, and spatial skills respond to training (Uttal et al., 2013, Psychological Bulletin). Orthogonal planes appear at lower grades; oblique planes, the classic source of egocentric error, appear from Registrar grade upward. Teaching tool only; not a psychometric instrument.
