Match the Slice

A two-dimensional slice is produced by the position of a plane moving through a three-dimensional object. Find the hidden target pocket and lock the alignment.

  1. Rotate the object: drag it directly, or use the arrow buttons below it.
  2. Zoom with scroll or the + and − buttons.
  3. Scrub the depth slider to move the slice, and the side slider to place the band.
  4. Watch the slice preview: the signal glows brighter as you close in. Lock when it peaks.
