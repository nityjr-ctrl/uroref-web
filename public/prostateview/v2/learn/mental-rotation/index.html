Mental Rotation

Choose the rotated lesion configuration from mirrored distractors before moving into plane thinking.

Evidence-backed 2 min

Prostate Spatial Localisation Drill

Lesion Rotation Trainer

One of the three lesion configurations below is the target rotated in three dimensions. The other two are mirror images. Select the rotated match. This is the same mental operation used when holding a multifocal lesion's arrangement in mind whilst moving between MRI planes, or relating a reported focus to its position in the gland.

Which configuration is the same lesion, rotated?

 

Evidence base. Task paradigm after Shepard & Metzler (1971, Science) and Vandenberg & Kuse (1978). A meta-analysis of 217 studies found spatial training produces durable, transferable gains (Uttal et al., 2013, Psychological Bulletin). Mental rotation ability correlates with technical and procedural skill acquisition (Wanzel et al., 2002, The Lancet). In prostate work, the same operation underlies relating multiparametric MRI planes to gland anatomy, localising reported lesions, and planning targeted biopsy. Teaching tool only; not a psychometric instrument.
