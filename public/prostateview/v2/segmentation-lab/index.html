    De-identified real MRI · Graded

The graded curriculum uses 30 unique de-identified Prostate158 studies: 3 whole-gland, 3 zonal, 12 lesion-delineation, 8 difficult-target and 4 advanced verification cases. Lesion cases teach boundary mapping of an assigned target; they are not detection or PI-RADS exercises.

Inside each case

A feedback loop that teaches.

The same sequence in every warm-up and every graded case, so the habit transfers.

  1. 01SweepReview every displayed slice before drawing. The reference stays hidden.
  2. 02CommitOn warm-ups, record what you think is there and how sure you are, before the tools unlock.
  3. 03TraceFreehand trace or place points, slice by slice, with copy-to-next-slice and full undo.
  4. 04CompareSubmit to reveal the case-specific educational reference as a colour-coded overlay.
  5. 05CorrectReturn straight to the flagged slices, resubmit, and compare attempts side by side.
  6. 06Review in 3DAfter scoring, open the protected same-study 3D model for spatial context.

Measured, not marked

Feedback a trainee can act on.

Scores describe agreement with the case-specific educational reference. Thresholds are teaching heuristics, not competence standards.

Dice and IoU
Whole-stack and per-slice overlap with the reference.
Boundary error
95th-percentile and mean in-plane distance, in millimetres.
Displayed-stack volume
Learner and reference volume with signed difference.
Regional agreement
Inferior, middle and superior thirds reported separately.
Structured findings
Deterministic rules name the pattern, why it matters and the slices to revisit.
Attempt history
Every attempt on a case is kept and compared on this device.

Built to be defensible

Clear scope. Visible limits.

Governance is part of the product. Every authenticated page carries its limitations, and the full governance record is one click away inside the Lab.

Real, licensed imaging

Every graded case is a unique de-identified Prostate158 T2 study (CC BY 4.0), with attribution and an indication of changes recorded.

Protected delivery

Images and 3D models are served only inside an authenticated session, hash-verified, never cached and never placed in the public site.

Reference withheld until submission

No answer geometry reaches the browser before an attempt is scored on the server.

Honest about what it is

An educational reference from one published reader, not a consensus contour. No AI inference runs anywhere in the Lab.

Not a credential

Practice XP and progress live only on the learner's device. Scores describe agreement with a reference, not clinical competence.

Scope stated on every page

Not a diagnosis, not a replacement for radiological review, not for biopsy planning or live decisions.

For invited reviewers and partners

Ready to look inside?

Access is by password, issued to invited clinical and educational reviewers. A session lasts six hours and can be locked at any time. Progress stays on your device and is never uploaded.
